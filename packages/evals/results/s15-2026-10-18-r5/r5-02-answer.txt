Most episodes start right after ciod: LOGIN chdir(...) failed: No such file or directory. That means a job was launched with a working directory that didn’t exist, for example /p/gb2/draeger/benchmark/dat32k_060205. My background reading, which the logs don’t prove: ciod fails the login, the control socket to the front end then closes, and every I/O node in the partition logs the read failure.

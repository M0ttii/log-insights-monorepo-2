## Answer
**This is not one bad node. The failure looks job-wide or caused by a shared component.** In the selected burst, the same message hit every rack in the scope within a single minute. Across the month, no single node stands out. Where the failure comes from is still open: one shared component, such as the control-stream endpoint all the messages name, could produce these broad symptoms.

### Next check
- **What to look at:** the job/partition logs and the service at `172.16.96.116`, outside this corpus. Check for a job kill, a control-stream disconnect, or a restart of that service around 2005-06-04T07:24:32Z [r_ac1295db.f1] and the other active days.
- **If a matching job end or service event exists on each active day:** the shared-component hypothesis gains support but is still not proven. A separate test (e.g. that service's own error log) would be needed to tell it apart from a normal job teardown.
- **If no such event exists:** the shared-endpoint idea gets weaker, but the job/partition explanation stays open.
- **In the corpus:** an hourly event_trend on each active day, split by rack, to see whether every burst starts at the same moment everywhere.

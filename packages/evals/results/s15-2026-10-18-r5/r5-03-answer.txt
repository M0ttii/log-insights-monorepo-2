It arrives in synchronized bursts, and every line points at the same control-stream peer, 172.16.96.116. That pattern points to a job-launch or shared-filesystem/service-node issue rather than a bad compute or I/O node. The logs only show that association, though; they don’t prove the cause. A single failing component, such as the host at 172.16.96.116, could still produce symptoms this broad.

Hypotheses
• Symptoms confined to one bad node: contradicted. This assumes the errors come from a single node. The breadth check found 512 nodes [r_a53a577a.f12], which refutes that.
• Job launch fails because the working directory is missing or unreachable (filesystem/mount problem), so ciod’s control connection breaks: supported, not proven. The evidence for it is the LOGIN chdir precursor in 6 [r_324ac096.f6] of 7 [r_324ac096.f1] burst starts. Against it, one burst had no such precursor, and only 1 [r_324ac096.f11] burst start had a LINKCARD bit-sparing event before it instead.
• The peer host at 172.16.96.116 (front-end or service node) dropped the control streams: open. Every line names that peer, but these logs contain no health data for it.
• A single failing node or component is the root cause despite the broad symptoms: open. No check so far refutes it.

Answer: It isn’t one bad node. The errors appear on many nodes at once and in recurring bursts, so the symptoms are job-wide or partition-wide. That makes the job’s launch environment (a missing working directory) or the shared peer the first thing to check. The cause is not proven. A single faulty component behind that peer could still produce symptoms this broad, so that origin also stays open.

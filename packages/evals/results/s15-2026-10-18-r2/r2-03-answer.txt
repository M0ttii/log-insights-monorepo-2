The next thing to check is whether the job’s working directory existed and was mounted on the I/O nodes at launch time.

Hypotheses
- A job-level launch problem (missing or unmounted working directory, so the job aborts and the control connections drop on every I/O node) is supported, but not proven. The breadth, the synchronized bursts, the shared peer, and the chdir precursor all fit it. The precursor is only a timing relationship, though.
- A single faulty node is contradicted. The error is spread across hundreds of nodes and dozens of racks, and the top node accounts for only a tiny share.
- The shared peer 172.16.96.116 (the front-end or service node) being the failing side is open. It’s consistent with the data, but nothing here tells it apart from the job-launch explanation. Widespread symptoms can still come from one shared component.
